LoopTodo Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026. Applies to the LoopTodo Android app (com.parker1978.looptodo).

TL;DR

To put it simply:

  • I don't collect data.
  • I don't share data.
  • LoopTodo has no internet access. Your lists never leave your device.

Overview

LoopTodo respects your privacy. The app does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data. Your lists, todos, tags, loop history and settings are kept in the app's private storage on your device.

Information Collection and Use

LoopTodo does not:

  • Collect personal information
  • Use analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools
  • Send data to external servers (it doesn't request Android's internet permission at all)
  • Require an account or sign-in
  • Display ads or use advertising SDKs

Everything you enter stays on your device and is never shared with anyone, including the app's developer.

Backups

LoopTodo is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. If you choose Settings → Export, the app writes a backup file to a location you pick with the Android file picker. Where that file goes afterwards (for example a cloud drive you choose) is up to you and covered by that service's privacy policy. Import reads a file you choose and replaces the app's data with its contents.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show reminders you set on todos.
  • Alarms & reminders: so reminders fire on time.
  • Run at startup: to restore reminders and scheduled list loops after your phone restarts.

None of these give LoopTodo access to your personal information.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because no data is collected, nothing is stored outside your device and nothing is retained by the developer. Delete todos or lists in the app, or uninstall LoopTodo (or clear its storage in Android settings), to remove all of its data.

Age Appropriateness

LoopTodo contains no objectionable content and is suitable for users of all ages. There are no in-app purchases, advertisements, or social features.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
